# My Favorite Movie: *A Walk to Remember*

**A Walk to Remember** is a 2002 romantic drama film based on Nicholas Sparks’ novel of the same name. It tells the story of Landon Carter and Jamie Sullivan, two high school students whose lives change after they grow closer.

## Why I Like It

- It has a meaningful and emotional story.
- The characters show kindness, faith, and personal growth.
- The soundtrack is memorable.
- The movie teaches that love can inspire people to become better.

## Main Characters

1. **Landon Carter** — A popular high school student who changes through his relationship with Jamie.
2. **Jamie Sullivan** — A kind, intelligent, and faithful student who deeply affects Landon’s life.
3. **Reverend Sullivan** — Jamie’s father and the local minister.

## Favorite Quote

> “Our love is like the wind. I can’t see it, but I can feel it.”
